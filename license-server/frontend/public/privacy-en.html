Privacy Policy

Information about data processing on the Editnhanh website and in the Facebook integration in Video Studio Tools.

Last updated: September 3, 2026. Privacy contact: doanhoang1910@gmail.com.

Operator: Đoàn Việt Hoàng. Editnhanh is operated by Đoàn Việt Hoàng.

1. Data used

  • Accounts and licenses: information you provide when registering, such as your name, email and phone number; account authentication information, subscription details, transactions and device identifiers used for activation, license management and support.
  • Facebook connection: information about Pages you authorize, including their IDs, names, profile pictures, categories, follower counts, permitted actions and Page Access Tokens. You sign in on Facebook; the application does not ask you to enter your Facebook password in an Editnhanh form.
  • Page management content: videos, descriptions, publishing schedules, post IDs and processing status; comment content, commenter names, likes and insight metrics returned by Meta under the permissions granted.
  • Website: session, device and IP data and authentication logs used to operate the service. The website integrates Meta Pixel and Conversions API to measure events such as visits, registrations, application downloads, contact requests and purchases; this data may include cookie identifiers, traffic sources and hashed contact information used to match events.

2. Purposes of use

Data is used to provide accounts and licenses, retrieve the Pages you manage, carry out publishing or scheduling actions you choose, display and manage comments, read insights, troubleshoot errors and provide support. When you enable publishing after rendering, the application creates a publishing job using the Page and settings you selected.

3. Where Facebook data is processed and stored

The hosted OAuth backend receives the login code and exchanges it for tokens with Meta. The Meta App Secret stays on the backend. The connection result is encrypted and stored temporarily for delivery to the device that started the session.

The result delivery session expires after 10 minutes. The temporary token data is removed from the session when the device acknowledges that it has saved it. If there is no acknowledgment, MongoDB removes the expired record through its TTL mechanism, which may run after the expiration time. The API no longer allows results to be retrieved from an expired session.

Page information and tokens are stored encrypted on the computer running the application. Videos, content and publishing job history are stored locally. When publishing, the application sends the video and content directly to Meta. Comments and insights are retrieved for display in the management dialog.

4. Processing locations and data recipients

  • Meta: processes Facebook login, Page access permissions, published content, comment actions and insights. The website also sends measurement events through Meta Pixel and Conversions API as described in section 1.
  • Render: provides the infrastructure running the website and OAuth/licensing backend. Connection requests, license verification and the data needed to process requests pass through this backend.
  • MongoDB database: stores account and license data and temporarily encrypted OAuth results. Backend data has a different scope from the videos and job history on your computer.
  • Google Gmail: processes messages you send to doanhoang1910@gmail.com for support and data requests. Information you include in those messages is used to verify and resolve your request.
  • Transactional email services and tools you use: necessary information may be passed to the website's authentication/notification email service. For AI or other services you configure, content for processing may be sent to the corresponding service when you use that feature.

Connecting Facebook alone does not upload your source videos to the OAuth backend. Uploading a video to Facebook is a separate action that you choose or enable automatically.

5. Retention

Local tokens are kept until you remove the Page from the application or delete the application data. Removing a Page does not also remove local publishing history or videos. The job history limits the number of completed records, but there is no fixed deletion period for every job; unfinished jobs are kept so they can be checked and processing can continue.

Account, license, transaction and support request data is managed separately from Facebook connection data. When requesting deletion, you can specify the scope of the data to be handled. Support will confirm what can be deleted and what must be retained to resolve transactions or related obligations.

  • OAuth sessions: expire after 10 minutes; temporary token data is handled through the acknowledgment and record cleanup mechanisms in section 3. This is not the retention period for tokens on your computer or your Editnhanh account.
  • Authentication logs recorded by the application: the system has a cleanup task for records older than 90 days, scheduled every 24 hours while the server is running. Actual deletion may be later if the task is interrupted. This period does not apply to all logs held by infrastructure providers.
  • Accounts, transactions and support correspondence: there is currently no single automatic deletion deadline covering all these data groups. They are reviewed in response to deletion requests and the needs of licensing, transactions, support or related obligations. If some data must be retained, the outcome will identify the data, the reason and the retention period or condition for ending retention.
  • Data on your computers: removing a connection on one computer does not delete copies on other computers, backups or video files you have copied. You need to handle those copies where they are stored.

6. Controls and deletion requests

You can remove saved Pages, revoke app permissions in Facebook settings or request help with your data. See the data deletion instructions to distinguish local data, backend data and content already published on Facebook.

To request access, correction or deletion, contact doanhoang1910@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, Page Tokens, App Secrets or verification codes by email.

Deletion requests receive a response within 5 business days of receipt and are processed within 30 days after the necessary information has been verified. If more time is needed, you will be informed of the reason and estimated completion time before the deadline. See the deletion process, scope and timelines.

7. Policy changes

When data handling changes, the content and update date on this page will be revised so you can review it before using the relevant feature.
