Data Deletion Instructions

You can disconnect Facebook and request help with data stored by Editnhanh. The steps below have different scopes.

Operator: Đoàn Việt Hoàng. Editnhanh is operated by Đoàn Việt Hoàng.

1. Stop jobs and remove the Page on your computer

  1. In Video Studio Tools, open Quản lý Page (Page Management) and turn off Tự động đăng Facebook (Automatic Facebook Publishing).
  2. Check your publishing history. Cancel pending jobs where a Cancel button is available. For schedules Facebook has already accepted, open Meta Business Suite or Facebook to check and cancel them there if you wish.
  3. Click Xóa (Delete) on the Page you want to disconnect and confirm. This removes the Page record and token from the application's account store on the current computer.
  4. If you connected on multiple computers, repeat these steps on each one. Local videos, projects and job history are not deleted along with the Page.

2. Revoke access on Facebook

Sign in to Facebook and open the settings for applications or Business integrations. Find the app you used to connect Editnhanh and remove its access. The app name shown during Facebook login helps you identify the correct app. Settings labels may differ between accounts.

Revoking access on Facebook does not automatically remove files already saved on your computer. Removing a Page in the application also does not itself revoke the Meta App's permissions on Facebook.

3. Request deletion or assistance with local data

Email doanhoang1910@gmail.com with the subject Facebook data deletion request and include:

  • Your Editnhanh account email and the name or ID of the relevant Page.
  • The scope of your request: Facebook connection data, local job history or Editnhanh account data.
  • Contact information for receiving instructions and the outcome of your request.

Support will verify your authority to make the request before processing it and will inform you of the outcome or explain which data needs to be retained and why. Data stored only on your computer must be deleted on that computer following the instructions provided; sending an email does not automatically delete local data.

Do not send Facebook passwords, Page Tokens, App Secrets, OTPs or screenshots containing these details. You do not need to send entire videos to request deletion of connection data.

Response and processing times

Editnhanh responds to deletion requests within 5 business days of receiving the email. The response confirms the scope of the request and identifies any minimum additional information needed for verification.

Processing of data within Editnhanh's control is completed within 30 days after the necessary information has been verified. If more time is needed, Editnhanh will explain why and provide an estimated completion time before that deadline. If verification information is incomplete, the response will identify what is missing; the 30-day period has not yet started.

How requests are handled

  1. Confirm the account email, relevant Page and scope of data to delete. Only information necessary to verify the requester's authority is requested.
  2. Check for remaining backend data and provide instructions for handling data on each connected computer. Deleting local data requires your action or cooperation; the server does not automatically access or delete files on your computer.
  3. Delete data within the verified scope that can be processed. If some data must be retained to resolve transactions or related obligations, explain what is retained, why, and the retention period or condition for ending retention.
  4. Email the outcome, stating what has been processed, any actions you still need to take and any data retained. Published content and schedules already accepted by Facebook remain managed separately on Facebook.

4. OAuth session data on the server

Facebook connection results are stored temporarily in encrypted form. Token data is removed from the session when the application acknowledges that it has saved the result. Sessions expire after 10 minutes; MongoDB removes expired records through its TTL mechanism, which may run after the expiration time. Results cannot be retrieved from expired sessions.

5. Posts and videos already sent to Facebook

Disconnecting, deleting Editnhanh data or uninstalling the application does not automatically delete published posts, comments, videos or schedules already accepted by Facebook. Manage that content in Facebook or Meta Business Suite using an account authorized for the Page.
